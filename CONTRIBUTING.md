# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help. This repository is generated: the solutions, hints and notes are
maintained in a private repository and published here as obfuscated spoilers, so pull requests
cannot be merged directly. Issues are the way in.

## Useful issues

- **A local test is wrong** (the expected output disagrees with what the judge accepts, or a test
  breaks the statement's limits): name the problem and the test file.
- **A hint is confusing or gives away too much for its step**: name the problem and the step. Please
  do not paste the hint text or any solution in the issue: other people read issues before solving.
- **The tutor misbehaved**: it showed code, skipped your consent, cited something that is not in
  `data/references.json`, ran a command you did not ask for. Describe what you asked and what it did.
- **A broken reference** in `data/references.json`, or a better one for a technique.
- **Library bugs** in `lib/`, with a failing input.
- **Setup problems** with `scripts/verify.py` on your platform.

## Please do not

- Open pull requests or issues with solutions, decoded spoilers or editorial-style explanations.
- Report "the spoiler can be decoded": it can, on purpose. It is there so that you do not read a
  solution by accident, not to keep it secret.

## Giving up is fine

Your give-ups are recorded in `progress/give-ups.md` in your clone. That is part of practicing
honestly, not something to hide: if you push your fork, they go with it.
