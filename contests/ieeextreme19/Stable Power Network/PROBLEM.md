</p><p>However, not all links are equally reliable: some introduce a risk of message corruption, and all require a certain transmission time.  
</p><p>
</p><p>A message must be sent from server <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.64444em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span></span> to server <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.68333em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.10903em;">N</span></span></span></span></span>.  
</p><p>To evaluate the quality of a route, the company defines a pair of values:
</p><p>
</p><ul><li><strong>Maximum risk</strong> (<span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>m</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>x</mi><mi>R</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">maxR</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.68333em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">m</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal">x</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.00773em;">R</span></span></span></span></span>): the highest risk value among all links used in the route.
</li><li><strong>Total time</strong> (<span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>t</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>t</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>l</mi><mi>T</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">totalT</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.69444em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.01968em;">l</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.13889em;">T</span></span></span></span></span>): the sum of the transmission times of all links in the route.
</li></ul><p>
</p><p>The company prioritizes <strong>safety over speed</strong>. That is, among all possible routes, it prefers the one that first minimizes <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>m</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>x</mi><mi>R</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">maxR</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.68333em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">m</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal">x</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.00773em;">R</span></span></span></span></span>; and, in case of a tie, the one with the smallest <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>t</mi><mi>o</mi><mi>t</mi><mi>a</mi><mi>l</mi><mi>T</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">totalT</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.69444em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal">o</span><span class="mord mathnormal">t</span><span class="mord mathnormal">a</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.01968em;">l</span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.13889em;">T</span></span></span></span></span>.
</p><p>
</p><p>Your task is to determine the quality of the best possible transmission from server <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mn>1</mn></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">1</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.64444em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord">1</span></span></span></span></span> to server <span><span class="katex"><span class="katex-mathml"><math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">N</annotation></semantics></math></span><span class="katex-html" aria-hidden="true"><span class="base"><span class="strut" style="height:0.68333em;vertical-align:0em;"></span><span class="mord mathnormal" style="margin-right:0.10903em;">N</span></span></span></span></span>.  
</p><p>If no such route exists, you must report it.
